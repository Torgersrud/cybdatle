# Cybdatle

The domain language of Cybdatle: the daily "guess who" game played by the 2nd-year class,
forked from komtekle. These are the words the class and the codebase agree on.

## Language

**Cybdatle**:
The daily "guess who" game of the 2nd-year class, a fork and rebrand of komtekle.
_Avoid_: Komtekle (that is the upstream 5th-year game), the new game

**Core cast**:
The twelve founding characters of Cybdatle: the six rooms plus the six lecturers, all
introduced on the launch date.
_Avoid_: Starting roster, base cast

**Rom**:
A room character — a room treated as a member of the cast, with gender, height and all the
other columns like any person. The six are A3, Komtekloungen, Sahara, Mojave, Ngorongoro and Nomade.
_Avoid_: Room (in English), location, place

**Lecturer**:
A teaching-staff member of the cast: Thomas Zinner, Katrien, Tjerand, Poul, Orosj and Stas.
_Avoid_: Professor, teacher

**2. klasse**:
The 2nd-year class the game is played by and guessed about; its classmates make up the student
roster on top of the core cast.

**Launch date**:
2026-11-25, the day Cybdatle's fresh timeline begins; nothing is served before it.

**Fresh timeline**:
The fork's reset chronology: every entry is dated to the launch date, pick history starts
there, and komtekle's past days no longer constrain this game. The upstream no-edit
compatibility rule applies again from launch day onward.
_Avoid_: Backdated roster, continued timeline

**Verv-whipped**:
The joke column grading how entangled a character is with the class' verv (offices).

**Mystisk kolonne**:
Placeholder name for the successor of komtekle's "Certified mojavebabe" column; the class
has not yet picked its real name and Ja / Nei / cheeky-third-value split.
_Avoid_: Certified mojavebabe (retired with the upstream roster)

**Antatt kjønn**:
The perceived-gender column — what the guesser thinks, not an identity statement; rooms are
Inkjekjønn.

**Høyde-tier**:
One of the six ranked height levels, from Lommeformat up to Tårnhøyde.

**Ekvivalent svar**:
A guess whose every column matches the target but whose name does not — close, but not right.
